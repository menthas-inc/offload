offload is where somebody puts down whatever is on their mind so they never have to organise it: a typed line, a link, a photo, a voice note, a forwarded email. Each of those is a dump. offload files every dump into threads, one per subject they keep, like a trip, a renovation or the groceries, and some threads are shared with people they know. They never choose where something goes, and they can always move it. After a turn they see a Done mark on their message and a link to each thread something was written to, and they can ask offload about anything they have put in.
