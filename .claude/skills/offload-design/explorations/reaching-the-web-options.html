Reaching the web, and saying what it is doing

Designs for 016 and 017, from the brief in the handoff folder and the two feature specs. The footer is first because everything else is a chip, a row or a line and all three need somewhere to live. What is settled in the brief is drawn rather than reopened: the chip's two fields, the three chip cap, the outline tone, and both switches defaulting on. Three things here need your word and they are marked; the rest I have taken.

What this decides

read this first
  • The footer belongs to offload's side of the turn, left aligned, at the log's full gutter. The Done mark moves off the person's sent bubble to get there, which is the divergence the brief expects. Section 1.
  • Done, then threads, then sources. What happened, where it landed, where it came from. One order on all three surfaces, wrapping rather than scrolling, min-height.
  • The source chip is the one sanctioned external link in the product, and it is safe for a reason worth writing down: its label is a domain from the read record, not a word the page chose. Section 2.
  • The switch notes name the recipient. The default no longer carries the weight, so the sentence has to. Section 3.
  • The voice amendment is right but not as worded. "Its own limits" is too wide; the line that holds is first person for what offload will not or cannot do, third person for what it did. Section 4.
  • The progress line keeps the dots and sits beside them. One row: dots, line, and at ten seconds a clay Stop at the end of it. Section 5.

  • The label is the registrable domain and nothing else. No path, no title, no scheme, no www., and a 22ch max-width so only a genuinely long host truncates, from the end. A path is page-chosen text arriving in the row that is supposed to be a fact about where a request went.

  • It leaves the app. No confirmation sheet: a link that announces itself as a domain, tapped deliberately, is the least surprising thing in the row.

4. The voice

decided: no subject at all

Not the amendment the brief proposed and not the rewrite either. The refusal is "Can't search that.", and the rule behind it is that offload's replies drop the subject and start with the verb, so neither "I" nor "offload" appears. That turns out to be what most of the product already does; the three "I" lines and the one "offload" line are the exceptions. Contractions are in as of this pass, which is a second change: every shipped line writes "did not" and "could not" in full, so the clipped forms below are new on both counts.

Needs your call

from the log, merged today

Twelve entries landed in decisions.md from the code side since 9 September, carrying design impacts nothing in the kits has drawn. Ranked by what I think matters; the first three I would draw next.

  • The thread's Notes section has four states nobody has drawn: at zero, over the prompt budget, mid-fold, and reading folded history. The code side calls this the gap worth closing first and I agree, because "some of these are not reaching offload" is a sentence with no drawn treatment at all.
  • The timeline rail has no governed component on any surface. Day cell, week rail, month bar, and the dot cluster at the larger text size, which the entry says nobody has looked at on a device. --surface-noticed is new and wants a place on the palette page.
  • A reply can now hold a list, a quote and a link, and no kit draws any of them. The three rules are already decided, so this is drawing rather than deciding.
  • Blue now means three things on the rail: waiting on you, something on this day, and the day you are reading. The entry flags it for the next palette review. My read is that it holds because soft and solid separate the last two, but it is one hue doing a lot and worth your eye.
  • Checkbox needs a boolean variant, checked meaning on rather than done, with no strike-through. Asked for twice now, once by the calendar settings and once by the rail's repeat box, and answered locally both times.
  • MemoryRow wants renaming to NoteRow plus a date, a body indicator, a quiet over-budget state and a folded-history variant.
  • Small, settled, mechanical: the phone rows want the RowTools pair at 17px with --space-6 between them, drawn only where the row may be deleted; section headings want 22px controls a full gutter apart; the composer wants its inThread line, cap, counter and submit label. I can land all of these in the kits without a decision from you.
  • One latent divergence worth naming: the web draws its pencil unconditionally while the phones draw it only where the account may edit. On a shared thread those are different products. Code's to fix, but it is a design rule that is being stated twice.
