Thinking row

This replaces the three dots and the line Looking through your threads in a conversation. While offload works, the row is one small looping animation. When the model writes a thinking phrase, the phrase appears beside it and replaces itself as new ones arrive. From ten seconds, Stop joins the row. Ten animations from simple to insane, each 3 to 10 seconds and looping without a seam. Play a turn runs the real timing: the animation alone, a phrase from 2.5 seconds, Stop from 10.

Stop

  • Round is a 30px outlined circle holding an 8px ink square, the size of a thread pill, so it reads as part of the row rather than a link in it. The tap area runs 7px past it to 44.
  • Pill keeps the word, for anyone who does not read the square: Stop beside the same square, in the same 30px outline.
  • In the composer takes it out of the row. The send button turns into a filled stop while a turn runs, the place most chat apps put it, and the row is only the animation and the phrase. It cannot share the button with a new message, so a dump typed mid-turn waits for the turn or stops it.
  • Today's Stop is accent text with no shape. All three are ink: stopping is not something offload did, so it is not moss.

For every option

  • The row is the animation at 22px tall, a 10px gap, the phrase in 13.5px secondary, then Stop. It sits where the dots bubble sat, with no bubble: nothing in it is offload speaking.
  • A phrase replaces the last in 350ms, out upward and in from below with a 3px blur, never stacking. A screen reader hears each one politely. The animation is hidden from it; the row is labelled offload is thinking until a phrase arrives.
  • The animation never restarts when the phrase changes or Stop arrives. It runs from the turn's start to its end, then the row goes.
  • Reduced motion holds each one on a still frame, chosen to read as the mark rather than as a paused moment. Switch above to see them.
  • Dark mode needs no new colours for the ink ones, which take the ink token. The prism ones are drawn for light here and need a check on dark.
