A more immersive conversation, on the app as built

Idea 1 drawn over the phones as they are on main today: rows with no tools that open their sheet on a tap, the one edit sheet with When, Repeats and Labels inside it, the filter bar, the month, and the prism band behind the composer. What changes is the conversation sheet and the tab bar. Decided with the product owner on 30 September; the calls and what was picked are in immersive-conversation-decisions.html.

Read from apps/mobile at 70a5fa4: Conversation.tsx, Plate.tsx, ContentRow.tsx, RowEditSheet.tsx, LabelSheet.tsx, SheetFoot.tsx, FilterBar.tsx, Thread.tsx, lib/motion.ts. The prism here is the band texture under its graded veil, frozen as at rest; send something to see a stand-in for its arrival.

What changes

  • The sheet rises to 54px from the top, not to 78% of the screen, and the header row goes: no title, chevron or x, just the grabber.
  • No fold. Letting go past a quarter, or a flick down, closes the conversation; the plate is as it always is. The turns and the draft are kept, so tapping the composer brings the same conversation back.
  • The page behind recedes as it rises (scale 0.93 from the top, down 10px, a 14px corner), the scrim reaches 45% ink, and the tab bar slides down under the sheet. All of it follows the finger.
  • The tabs stay on a thread. Today the plate drops them there; here the thread keeps Today, My threads and Notes like every other screen, and they fold away only under the conversation or the keyboard.
  • One prism band at a time, as now: the plate's behind its composer and tabs at rest, the sheet's behind its own composer when it is up.
  • Every sheet drags the same way. The edit sheet gains the grabber and the conversation's physics; a drag closes it without saving, as Cancel does. The label sheet opened alone takes the same.
  • Only the conversation makes the page recede. Row sheets dim the page and leave it where it is.
  • The turn footer is as built: Done, the thread pill, the labels. The thread pill closes the conversation and opens the thread; a row is edited where it lives.
  • Android dims, and nothing recedes. The page stays where it is under a 55% scrim, a little deeper than iOS's 45% to make up for the missing depth; the sheet, the drag, the tabs and the close are the same. The status bar sits over the dimmed page as it does for any full-height M3 sheet, and predictive back drives the sheet down. In its own roles: 28px sheet tops, the M3 container colours, 8px chips at 32, the ink primary pill, the gesture bar.
  • Every sheet, not only the edit sheet: the label, When, action and custom repeat sheets take the same grabber and drag.
  • Phones only. Everything else on these phones is as built: tap a row for its sheet, Labels opens in place, the foot is Delete, Cancel, Save.
