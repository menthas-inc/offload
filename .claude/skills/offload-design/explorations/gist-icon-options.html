Gist, six icons

Each shown at tab-bar size with the label, and larger above it. The tab is a summary of everything across your threads, so the glyph should read as “the short version”, not as a home or a list.

Gisttext-quotein use

Lines with a quote bar, literally an extract. Closest to the word.

Gistscan-text

Text inside a frame, something read for you. Nods at the AI without sparkles.

Gistlayers

Everything stacked into one view. Abstract, and pairs well with the circle for threads.

Gistsun

A daily briefing. Warm, but says “today” more than “summary”.

Gistnewspaper

A digest. Clear, but busy at 22px and a bit publication-flavoured.

Gisthouse

The safe default. Says “landing page”, says nothing about a summary.

My pick is text-quote, it is the only one that means “the short version of something longer”, and its horizontal lines echo the list underneath. layers is the strongest abstract alternative and sits nicely beside the circle. Currently text-quote is wired into the app; say the word and I'll swap it.
