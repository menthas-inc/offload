offload on Android

Three ways to put the same product on Android. Same pages, same content, same brand colours; what changes is how much of Material 3 the app speaks. Each row shows Today, My things, a conversation, and Settings side by side.

Grounded in the current Material 3 spec, not memory: type scale (Title Large 22/28, Body Large 16/24, Body Medium 14/20, Label Medium 12/16), shape scale (4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 28, full for buttons and search), tonal elevation through surface-container roles rather than shadows, navigation bar 80dp with a secondary-container pill on the active item, top app bars at 64 / 112 / 152dp, FAB at elevation 3, sentence case for all UI text, and the Expressive additions (larger shapes at 20 / 32dp, emphasised weights, spring motion). Icons here are Material Symbols Rounded except in direction A, which keeps offload's current set.

Two questions the directions do not answer on their own. Icons: Android convention is Material Symbols, which is what B and C use here, but that means maintaining two icon sets; A keeps one set across both platforms. Dynamic colour: Material You can retint the app from the user's wallpaper. offload's identity is the black mark and the two accents, so my recommendation is to decline dynamic colour and keep the brand scheme, but it is a real platform expectation worth deciding deliberately.
