Today: telling the sections apart

The whole of Today with every section showing: Needs you, Your day, Reminders (C2b) and Open in your threads. Today all four draw grey cards on the page with a small heading, so the edges between them disappear. Five ways to separate them, and the current page for reference. Sunday 11 October.

The full page

all four sections, nothing scrolled
NowAs built
  • 15px headings and the same --surface-fill card in every section. Your day has no heading at all, only the stepper.
ABands
  • Sections alternate between the page and a full-bleed --surface-fill band; inside a band, cards turn white.
  • Every section gets a 20px heading, Your day included.
  • The strongest split for the least new vocabulary. Works with any order Customize Today keeps, since the alternation follows position.
BRules and big headings
  • A full-width --border-strong rule over each section, a 24px heading with its glyph, and the count at the right.
  • Cards stay as they are; the space between sections grows to 52px.
  • Editorial and calm. Separation comes from type and air, not colour.
CPanels
  • The page sinks to --surface-fill and each section becomes one white panel holding its heading and its items.
  • Inside a panel, items are separated by hairlines, not cards, so there is one container per section.
  • Open in your threads shows one page per panel; the dots stay.
DA colour for each
  • Each section gets a glyph tile and a tint from roles the tokens already give: blue for Needs you (this needs you), amber for Reminders (noticed), moss for Open in your threads (the accent). Your day stays ink.
  • Cards take the section's tint, so a glance at any card tells you where it sits.
  • The most distinct, and the most colour on a page that has used little.
EAn index on top
  • A sticky row of section chips with their counts under the app bar; tapping one scrolls to it, and the chip for the section in view fills.
  • Sections below get hairline rules and a dot in the same colour as the chip.
  • Good once Today is longer than a screen, which with all four it always is.
