Telling threads apart

Every thread on main wears the same lasso: in My threads, in a row's thread pill, in an answer's thread chip, in the Move picker. Past five threads the only way to find one is to read every title. Three ways to give each thread its own mark, all drawn over the same eleven threads, with the licence behind each. The mark sits wherever the thread is named, including the thread's own title, which today has none.

Now

one glyph for every thread

A. A glyph and a colour per thread

Lucide, ISC, already oursmy pick

offload picks a Lucide glyph from the thread's name when it makes the thread, and one of the ten label hues; you can change either. The mark is the glyph in the hue on a soft tile of the same hue. It keeps every rule the system has: same library, same 2px outline, same mask, no new licence. Lucide has about 1,600 glyphs; the picker offers a curated set of around 200 in eight groups, plus search. Shape does the recognising and colour does the finding, so it still works for somebody who cannot tell two hues apart. Second frame: glyph only, no colour, for comparison.

B. A flat emoji per thread

Fluent Emoji Flat, MIT

The idea as you put it: Microsoft's Fluent Emoji in its Flat style, about 3,100 drawings under MIT, bundled with the app so a thread looks the same on an iPhone, a Pixel and Windows. The most recognisable of the three, and the warmest. It breaks two lines of the readme (no emoji, no colour in icons), so it would have to be a written exception: the thread mark is the one place an emoji appears, always on a neutral tile, never in running text or as a reaction. Its full colour also competes a little with the label colours inside a thread.

C. Colour only

no library

The cheapest change: every thread keeps the lasso and takes a hue, on the tile and as a dot in its pill. No picker beyond ten swatches. It helps when you already know a thread's colour, and stops helping past ten threads, when two share a hue, or for anyone with colour blindness. Shown for completeness.

The same in every direction

  • offload picks, you change. The mark is set when the thread is made, from its name, the way the name is. Tap the mark in the thread's header (or Change icon on the web, and in the thread menu) for the picker: offload's six suggestions first, then search, then groups.
  • One mark per thread, for everyone in it. A shared thread looks the same to Ana and Tom. Changing it is a thread edit, like renaming.
  • Where it shows: My threads (40px), the thread's app bar beside the title (30px) and its header (64px), the thread pill on Today rows and answers (14px, replacing lasso), the Move picker and Move suggestions, People's shared threads, the web's thread list (32px). The tab glyph stays lasso.
  • Not drawn here, could pair with any of them: pinning threads to the top of My threads, and type-to-filter in its search. Both shorten the list rather than mark it.

To decide

  • Direction: A, B or C. My read: A, glyph and colour. It does what the emoji would, stays inside the design system, and costs no new licence.
  • If A: colour on or off.
  • Whether to pair it with pinning or type-to-filter.
