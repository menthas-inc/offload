A thread's map

Final view for approval. A thread with at least one point (a saved place, a link with a location, an event with a venue, offload's or a connected calendar's) gets a List | Map switch in its app bar. On the web, a window 1200px or wider shows the map as a column beside the list instead. The map is MapTiler Streets on every surface, drawn by MapLibre.

The tiles here are OpenStreetMap's standard style standing in for MapTiler Streets, which needs a key to preview. Pins, callouts, chips and the sheet are offload's own, so they look exactly like this on MapTiler. Every frame is live: tap the switch, a pin, the map, Open.

3 · Rules

What puts a pin
A saved place; a link with a location (offload reads it from the page: an Airbnb, a restaurant, a hotel); an event with a venue, offload's own or from a connected calendar the thread shows. A place offload could not locate is not drawn and stays in the list as it is.
Approximate places
A listing that only gives an area until it is booked (Casa Alfama here) is a soft circle in the label's colour instead of a pin: 16% fill, a 1.5px ring at 70%, about 300m across; picked, the ring is 2.5px and the fill 26%. Its callout sits on the centre, and the sheet's Address reads Approximate, Alfama. It becomes a pin when an exact address arrives.
The switch earns
When at least one live thing in the thread has a point. Losing the last one removes the switch and returns the thread to List. The Places section in the list is retired: located things sit in Everything in here like anything else.
Pins
16px dots with a white ring, 24px when picked, each inside a 44px target. The label's colour; two labels split down the middle; three or more show the first two; none is ink. Events are rounded squares. Overlapping pins group into an ink count dot until zoomed apart.
Callout
Name, kind and labels, then Directions and Open. Tapping the map closes it; tapping another pin moves it.
Directions
Opens the device's maps app with the place as the destination: Apple Maps on iOS, or a choice when Google Maps is installed; Google Maps on Android; Google Maps in a new tab on the web.
Open
The thing's sheet, the same as tapping its row: a half sheet over the map on the phones, dimming it at 35%; the side pane on the web. Header with the type tile, name, kind and thread, and a close. Rows for When, Address, Link, Labels and Added by. Two equal buttons: Directions filled, and Open link for a link or Edit otherwise.
Filtering
The chips are the thread's own filter, with the same pick in list and map. A filter that leaves no pins shows the thread's whole area with Nothing under Food on the map.
Framing
The map opens fitted to every pin, never closer than street level. A picked pin's callout is kept inside the frame.

Stack
MapLibre on all three (maplibre-gl-js; @maplibre/maplibre-react-native in an Expo dev build), MapTiler Streets, and Streets Dark in the dark theme.
