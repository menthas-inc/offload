Where the conversation appears

You typed “what do I still owe money for?” on Today. The answer has to arrive without losing the page it came from. All four keep the composer where it is, keep the page visible behind, and offer the same two moves: fold it away, or open it as a full conversation.

A. Sheet over the page

A rounded sheet rises to 65% and the page dims behind it. Two controls in its header: fold down, and open full. Swiping the grabber does the same thing. Most familiar; also the heaviest layer.
