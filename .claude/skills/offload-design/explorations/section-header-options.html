My read. Option 3 or 6. Both fix all three of your complaints: sentence case at 17px semibold in ink is larger and darker than the row titles it introduces, and the hairline gives the eye a real edge so the calendar stops colliding with the header above it. Option 3 is the firmer of the two; option 6 is lighter and leaves room for a right-hand action. Option 2 is the most obvious hierarchy but eats a lot of height on a screen with five sections. Option 4 works but adds a filled band, which is more chrome than anything else in the system.
