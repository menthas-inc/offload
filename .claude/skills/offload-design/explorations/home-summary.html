Home: what is open

Today is built around dates. With no dated to-dos it shows the day's events and little else, while the open to-dos sit inside threads. Four ways to add a section that summarises what is open and not dated. Each sits under the day, on Today only, as a fourth section in Customize Today.

Now

no dated to-dos, nothing needs you
Today as it is

Behind it: 14 open to-dos in 4 threads, 2 people you are waiting on, 9 things not in a thread.

A. By thread

a row per thread with open to-dos
Open in your threads
  • Each row names the thread, its next to-do and how many are open. A tap opens the thread at its to-dos.
  • Ordered by the thread with the oldest open to-do. Threads with none are left out; loose things close the list.
  • Closest to My threads, so it reads as familiar. It tells you where work is, less what it is.

B. Counts

four tiles, each opens a list
The tiles
Open to-dos, tapped
  • Open to-dos, waiting on others, not in a thread, threads that moved. A tile with zero is not drawn.
  • The most compact, and it covers kinds Today has no other place for. Numbers alone can feel like a backlog score.

C. offload's read

a short paragraph, on top
Above the day
  • The same voice as a thread's opening read, written for the whole account. Thread names are pills; two actions under it.
  • Can say what a list cannot: which thread is heaviest, who is waiting. Costs a model call per refresh, and it is easy to skim past.

D. Thread cards

a sideways strip, like the events
Open in your threads
  • One card per thread with its first three open to-dos, ticked off in place. The last card counts loose things.
  • Repeats the events strip above, so the page has one shape. Only two cards are in view; the rest need a swipe.
