Asking back, and arranging by hand

Nine items from handoff/FROM-CODE-2026-09-16.md, drawn at real size and decided item by item on 16 September. Six were decisions, where code left the visual open, or where what it built conflicted with a governed contract, or where drawing it at real size turned up something the entry did not know; three were approvals. This is the record of all nine rather than a request: the readme, the log entry and handoff/APPLY-2026-09-16.md are written. Each section head carries its outcome and each option carries what happened to it, so the ones that lost are still here with the reason.
