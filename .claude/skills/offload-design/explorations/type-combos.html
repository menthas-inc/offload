Five type combinations

The core face carries the interface; the second face is offload speaking. Each option below shows both, then the same three moments, an inbox, an answer, an empty state, so you can judge them where they will actually be read.

What changed already. The monospace face is gone: numerals are the core sans with tabular figures. The assistant voice is upright, never italic. Title-2 has been dropped from the scale. All faces here are Google Fonts, since there is no licence to work from, so any of these can ship today, and any can be swapped for a licensed face later.

01

chosensturdy, neutral, quietly bookish

Literata was built for long-form reading on screens: low contrast, thick strokes, big x-height. Against a tight grotesque it sounds settled and unhurried, an assistant that has already finished thinking.

one face, no second voice

05

Plus Jakarta Sans + Vollkorn

rounder, softer, more human

The one option that changes the core face too. Plus Jakarta Sans is geometric but soft-cornered, noticeably friendlier than a neutral grotesque; Vollkorn is a sturdy, slightly rounded serif with real warmth. Included as the counter-argument: if offload should feel gentle rather than precise, this is what that looks like.

Decided 11 August: option 01, Instrument Sans + Literata. Kept as the record of what it was weighed against.

My read at the time. 02 (Source Serif 4) is the closest fit to what you described, friendly, easy on the eye, no newsletter in it, and it fixes the one real complaint about Literata, which is weight. 04 (sans only) is the option I would take seriously if you want maximum discipline; it is the most consistent with a product whose whole promise is hiding complexity, and it costs you a webfont rather than gaining one. 03 is fine but keeps a trace of the thing you rejected. 05 is a different brand, worth looking at once, then probably setting aside.
