The intro, shared by all three

replaces the three tutorial screens

One screen that plays itself. The copy is a draft, approved for now. Three scenes of about ten seconds, with story-style bars at the top. It moves on by itself, and tapping the art jumps ahead. Continue is the only button and leaves from any scene. Each scene acts out its sentence: four loose dumps are filed one by one into the two threads they belong to, Lisbon trip and Bookshelf build, each count ticking up as one lands; a thread gains Ana and Tom and Tom adds something; a photo, a voice note, an email and a link all land in the same thread. The prism blooms behind the art only, and the copy sits on the page below it. With reduced motion the scenes cut without animating. Help's See the tutorial again plays this screen and nothing else.

The flow

after sign in
Intro→Before you start: name and consent→Bring things in: Mail, Calendar, notifications→Today

The name joins the consent: both are required and they are the only things first run asks. Mail, Calendar and notifications are three cards on one optional screen, each with its own button. The notification dialog fires from its card, not after consent. Connecting Calendar comes back to this screen with a Connected pill. A card that cannot apply is not drawn: Mail for an Apple hidden email, Calendar when the connector is off, notifications on the web. With no cards left the screen is skipped.
