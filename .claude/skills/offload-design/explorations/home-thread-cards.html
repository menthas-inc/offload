Home: thread cards

Five takes on D from Home: what is open, changing how a thread and its to-dos are drawn. Same data in each: 14 open to-dos in 4 threads, 9 things not in a thread.

1. Checklist strip

D as it was, tickable
One to-do just ticked
  • The thread pill and count on top, the first three open to-dos as a checklist, then how many more.
  • Ticking strikes the to-do through, and the next one moves up when you leave Today.
  • Densest of the five. Every card is the same height, so the strip reads as one row.

2. Next up

one to-do per thread
Each card leads with one to-do
  • Each thread shows only its next to-do, large, with who added it and how long it has been open.
  • Done and + Today act on it in place; the next one takes its card.
  • Asks for one decision per thread. The other open to-dos are only a count.

3. Thread pages

one wide card at a time, with its read
First page of four
  • A page per thread: its name, the people in it, offload's one-line read, then up to four to-dos with their age.
  • Swipe for the next thread; the dots count them. Next to the thread's own page, it is a preview.
  • Most context per thread. Only one thread is in view at a time.

4. Stacked threads

a vertical list, one open
The first thread open
  • Threads as full-width rows: name, count, people and the next to-do. A tap opens the row in place to its open to-dos.
  • The thread with the oldest open to-do comes first and is opened by default.
  • Every thread is in view without a swipe; the page grows long when one is opened.

5. Grid

two columns, counts first
All threads at a glance
  • A square per thread: pill, how many are open, and the next to-do at the foot. Loose things take the last square.
  • Reads at a glance and fits six threads before the page scrolls. Only one to-do per thread is named.
