Immersive conversation: decisions for you

Six calls that came out of drawing idea 1 over the app as it is on main. Decided on 30 September: 1B, 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A, 6A. The picks are folded into immersive-conversation.html and the handoff.

1. What a release does: fold or close

Today the sheet has three states: raised, folded to a row on the plate above the composer (the conversation's title and an x), and closed. Pulling it down folds it; the x closes it. Idea 1 took the header row out of the sheet, so it no longer says what folding is folding into.

2. Does the edit sheet drag like the conversation?

Today the edit sheet has no grabber and slides in and out on a timing; it closes from the scrim, Cancel or Save. The label sheet opened alone has a grabber but does not follow a drag either.

3BThe row in the footer, its sheet on top

The footer names the row, and a tap opens its edit sheet stacked on the conversation, which steps back a level. Close it and you are back in the conversation. Two levels at most. Needs the turn to carry the row's id and a fourth kind of footer chip.
