Prism, the design

The settled version, on the two surfaces it ships to. A single prism field sits behind the bottom plate in the app and behind the lockup on sign in, frozen, holding one warped still. When offload files something the real shader runs for two seconds, eases its clock to a stop, and freezes again wherever it landed. Nothing runs while the phone sits on a table, and no two arrivals leave the field in the same place. Density 140%, and the dump nudge 15% harder than the version you reviewed.

Arrival Both fields are frozen until something arrives. The shader runs for two seconds, slows, and stops.

In the app

The field is clipped to the plate, so it never reaches the list, and the only text over it is the three tab labels: the composer's placeholder sits on its own opaque pill. The fill above the field is graded rather than flat, light along the plate's rounded top edge where nothing sits and full by the label band, so the colour reads as a rim around the composer. Press Dump something: the row arrives with the change mark, the field nudges, and it warps for two seconds underneath. Scrolling slides it inside the plate, which is the one thing here that moves without offload having done anything.

The spec

what to build
 Value
FieldOne baked prism texture per surface: p2-band-1.png clipped to the plate, p2-bloom-1.png behind the lockup. 512×176 and 320×320, 117KB and 90KB as PNG, roughly half that as WebP.
Density140%. The field composites at 0.36 × 1.4 over --surface-page.
The fill over itGraded, not flat: 38% white at the plate's top edge, held to 30% of its height, reaching 86% by 60%. The tab labels sit below that, on the full 86%. Flat 86% everywhere left the field at a sixth of the gate's strength with no density setting able to recover it, since the fill eats whatever density adds.
At restFrozen. One warped still at a fixed clock. No timer, no loop, no shader context awake.
On arrival2000ms of live shader, with the clock eased to a stop over the final 700ms so the flow slows rather than cuts. The field stays where it landed, so the next arrival starts somewhere new.
The nudgeThe field translates -14.95px, -5.75px and scales to 1.046 at 26% of --duration-mark, then settles. 15% harder than the reviewed version. The plate itself does not move.
The gateSame field, same density, warps once on appear. No arrival exists there, so the screen's own appearance is the trigger.
ContainmentThe field is a child of the plate, so its box is the plate's box at any height and the rounded top corners clip it. It cannot reach the list, which is the only place --text-tertiary would meet it, and that colour has no contrast headroom.
Reduced motionThe shader never runs. Both surfaces hold the frozen still, and the nudge and the change mark are cut.
BackgroundedAny run in flight is cancelled on visibilitychange. Because nothing loops at rest, there is nothing else to stop.
  • Skia is the dependency, and this is the cheapest honest way to have it. A runtime shader adds several MB to the bundle, and the reason it usually costs battery is the render loop staying awake. Here it is awake for two seconds after somebody puts something down, which is also the only moment the distortion means anything.
  • The warp is the change mark in a second place. The row takes a moss ground settling over --duration-mark; the field takes two seconds of flow. Both say that offload just did something, and they fire together rather than as two effects that happen to coincide.
  • The app's field is quiet by design and the gate's is not. Same texture, same density: behind the plate most of it is under an 86% fill and three tab labels, and on the gate it is the open upper half of the screen. The asymmetry is the point, since one screen has content to protect and the other does not.
  • Two surfaces diverge from their contracts, and only two: the plate wrapper in App.jsx goes from --surface-raised to 86%, and the gate gains a field behind SignInScreen. Everything else on both screens is as specified, including the app bar's opaque chrome, the composer's --surface-input pill and TabBar's transparent fill.
  • What is not decided is dark theme. Every wash in the texture is a light tint chosen against paper, and over #1C1B19 they read as fog. Dark needs its own bake at a lower density, or the field is a light-theme feature and the readme says so.
