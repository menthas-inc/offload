The things themselves, never a thread. A group called Not in a thread sits between current threads and Older threads. Rows show their type tile and no thread name. Type chips sit under the header, only for types that are present, and filter the group in place: still three rows, then All 5 links. All 14 opens the full list.

  • Cannot be opened, renamed, shared or deleted as a whole. Swipe on a row moves or deletes that one thing.
  • Elsewhere, a row that shows its thread (Today, search) shows nothing in that slot.
  • Empty: the group, chips and All link disappear. One type only: no chips.
  • Swipe deletes, as every row's swipe does. Moving by hand is the row sheet's Move.
  • offload's reply when it files without a thread is Saved. No thread is named, because there is none.

2 · A suggestion on the row

A

A line under the row, and the whole line is one button. Tap anywhere on it to accept; the cross beside it dismisses. The thread name inside is a label, not a link.

  • Move to an existing thread: moss soft ground and the lasso, the thread reference's own colours.
  • Create a new thread: paper with a dashed moss outline, a plus, and the words "new thread". Only offered when two or more loose things belong together.
  • Where they come from: Move to from the planner at filing time, when it was not confident enough to file. Create from a daily job that groups loose things.
  • Only when offload is confident. Most rows have none. A dismissed suggestion does not come back for that thing.
  • Moss, because offload is suggesting. Nothing moves until you tap.

What changes

  • Storage is unchanged. Loose things stay in the account's default thread (accounts.default_thread_id), under its key. That thread is never drawn as a thread: not in My threads, not as a pill, not in the move sheet's list. Its contents are the group.
  • One new thing per item: an optional suggestion, either an existing thread id or a proposed name with the ids it would hold, plus a dismissed mark.
  • Older threads is about time. Slow threads sink and come back up when something lands.
  • Always on goes: kind:"always" in fixtures, the accent header in three kits, and its mention in the readme's moss role.
  • Kits: ui_kits/mobile_app/LooseParts.jsx, shared by all three, and OFFLOAD_DATA.loose.
