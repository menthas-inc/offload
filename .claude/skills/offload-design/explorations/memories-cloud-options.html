Memories — cloud icons

A cloud is a good instinct: it is the softest shape in the icon set, and it says “everything is up there, you don’t have to hold it” — which is the whole product. One caution worth naming: a cloud also means cloud storage and sync, so some people will read the tab as a settings or backup screen rather than their own memories.

Memories cloudrecommended

The plain single cloud. Soft, calm, and it fits the brand’s austerity better than any of the others here.

Memories cloudy

Two overlapping clouds. Reads as “several things gathered”, which suits a store of many memories — but busier at 22px.

Memories cloud-sun

Warmer and more scenic. Starts to feel like a weather app rather than a memory.

Memories cloud-moon

Night-time, dreamlike. Pretty, but too poetic and hard to read small.

Memories cloud-download

Pulling something back down out of the cloud — retrieval, literally. Also unmistakably a download button.

Memories cloud-cog

Cloud infrastructure. Wrong register entirely; included so you can rule it out.

My pick is the plain cloud. It is the only one calm enough to sit beside a hand-drawn lasso without the bar getting noisy, and at 22px it stays a clean silhouette. cloudy is the more meaningful of the two — many things gathered — if you can live with the extra detail. Rule out cloud-download (reads as a download button) and cloud-cog (infrastructure).
