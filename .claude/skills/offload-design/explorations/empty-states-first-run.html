The first screen with nothing in it

Get started lands on Today, and Today is empty. This is the screen that decides whether anyone dumps a first thing. Three ways were drawn and the first one is built: it is in all three kits behind the day one control in each preview bar. Then the other two tabs, which are empty at the same moment and should not each invent their own answer, and what Today looks like once one thing is in. Mobile is the iOS kit's chrome, Android is the same with the Material bars.

Rules for every empty state in the product

  • Never a zero. "Nothing in here yet", not "0 things". Counts appear only when there is something to count.
  • One sentence, two at most, in the serif voice, with a full stop. No headline plus body plus button stack.
  • First time and emptied are different screens. A new account gets the invitation and the examples. Someone who cleared everything gets one line, "Nothing left in here", and no teaching, because they know how it works.
  • No illustration, ever. The mark at 22% is the ceiling, and only on My threads and Memories.
  • The composer stays. Every empty screen in the app keeps it, because on every one of them the answer is to dump something.
  • Search has no empty state any more: asking happens in the composer, and offload answers in words when it finds nothing.
