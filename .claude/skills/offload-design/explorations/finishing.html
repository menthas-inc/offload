Finishing: launch, the keyboard, the kit

The three design items the 30 September build left open. 1 and 2 are built on main already; the options are whether to keep what was built or change it before the kits draw it. 3 is a scope call for the mobile kit, in the form.

1. Launch, when nothing answers

only a 401 signs you out

Since 30 September a failed /api/me holds on launch instead of showing the gate. The mark stays where the splash put it, measured from the screen's centre, and the failure takes the spinner's place, 64px under the mark. No plate.

L1. As built: one line, Try again beside it
L2. Keeps trying, quietly
L3. Says whose side it is on
  • L1. That didn't go through. in --text-danger, Try again in accent beside it: the line the people and invitation screens already draw when a read fails. Nothing happens until it is tapped.
  • L2. The spinner stays and offload retries on its own, 2, 4, 8 then every 15 seconds, under a quiet Can't reach offload. Still trying. in --text-secondary. Try again appears as a pill after 30 seconds. The 30 September outage was nine minutes at the edge; this recovers from it with no tap and says nothing alarming.
  • L3. Two cases, two titles. No network: You're offline, offload picks up when you're back., and it retries when the connection returns. Network but no answer: offload isn't answering, It's on our side. Trying again., retrying as L2 does, with a Try again pill. The most honest, and two strings to keep true.

2. The plate under the keyboard

its shape never changes

As built: the keyboard rises over the tabs first, and the plate starts riding it only when the keyboard's edge reaches the composer. Three moments of the rise, then one alternative.

K1a. At rest
K1b. Rising: the tabs go under the keyboard
K1c. Up: the plate rides it
K2. Alternative: the tabs fade as it rises
  • K1, as built. Nothing on the plate moves or re-lays out; the keyboard simply covers what is below the composer, and the prism band rides up with the composer. One motion, and it is the platform's.
  • K2. The same geometry, with the tabs fading to nothing over the first 60px of the keyboard's rise, so no label is ever cut by the keyboard's edge mid-rise. A small polish on top of K1, not a different model.
  • The web has no on-screen keyboard; it is unchanged.
