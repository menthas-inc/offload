The first tab — ten names

Each name with the icon that fits it, shown in the real bar beside My things and Memories. Worth remembering what the screen does: it summarises what moved across everything since you last looked, and flags the one thing waiting on you. That is a briefing, not a home page — so the best names are about time or the short version, not about place.

My picks. Today is the one I would ship: it describes the content exactly, needs no explanation, and the sun glyph is the calmest thing that could sit in that slot. Catch up is the best if you want the tab to feel like an action rather than a place. Loose ends is the most distinctive and the most on-brand, but it greets you with a problem every time. The gist is still good — it just reads as a label someone chose, where Today reads as a fact.
