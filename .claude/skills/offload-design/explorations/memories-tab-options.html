Memories, four directions

Every option has the same two sections and the same behaviour: a memory pill opens what offload knows on that subject, a conversation pill reopens that chat and the composer appends to it. They differ in how the memories are ordered, and therefore in what the screen says about you at a glance.

AOne field of pills

No grouping. Every subject in one wrap, sized by how much offload knows: the things you talk about most are physically bigger. Conversations underneath as a plain list.
