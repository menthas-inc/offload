The folded conversation

Same moment in all four: you asked about money, offload answered, and you folded it away. The page is back, the composer is back, and the conversation has to stay reachable without floating loose above the footer.

1. The footer keeps the sheet

The sheet does not leave, it shrinks to its own header. Same rounded top, same grabber, same shadow, with offload’s last line where the transcript was. The composer and tabs sit inside it, so footer and conversation are one object.
