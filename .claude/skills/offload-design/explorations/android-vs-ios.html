iOS and Android, page by page

Direction A with your two changes: the active tab is now the deeper blue with a white glyph, and lists and section headers follow B, so no ink underline and every group sits in its own container. One icon set across both platforms, and no dynamic colour.

Both frames are drawn at 360dp so the comparison is about layout rather than width. Android side follows the current Material 3 spec: 64dp start-aligned top app bar with Title Large, 80dp navigation bar with the active pill, 56dp minimum list rows, Body Large titles with Body Medium supporting text, containers on the surface-container-low role at 12dp, and a 4dp snackbar. Surfaces are neutral greys, not moss-tinted. Every string, count and badge in both columns is read from the kit's own data.js, so the iOS side is the product rather than a drawing of it.

Two things I did not change, so flag them if you disagree. The composer keeps its blue rim on Android rather than becoming a plain filled search bar, because that rim is now part of the brand. And the active tab pill uses blue-600 with a white glyph as you asked, which is stronger than Material's usual secondary-container tint; the label under it takes blue-700 so the pair reads as one selected item.
