## docs/tech/decisions.md

### 2026-10-10 · Reminders: a model labels, a table schedules, and nothing is scheduled ahead

offload reminds a person before the dates that need it, and they choose only off, how many (one, two or three) and, per thing, a stop. **The model never picks a time.** The nightly pass (032) answers two closed questions about each dated thing of its member's, what kind of date it is (meeting, appointment, occasion, deadline, trip, day, none) and how much it needs beforehand (none, days, weeks, months), and the memory pass (038) dates a birthday or an anniversary it holds. One pure function, `notifications/plan.ts`, maps a label, a date, the account's clock and How many to at most three moments, a month out, a week out and on the day, with the order How many keeps them in fixed per kind and lead. The table came from a survey of reminder timing (appointment and bill payment trials, calendar studies): the nearest reminder carries most of the value when nothing is needed beforehand, about a day before is best for somewhere you go, and an early reminder earns its place only when something has to be bought, booked or applied for.

**Nothing is scheduled ahead.** A label holds no date; the two sends work out at the moment they go which reminders land in them, as 024's digests already did, and the hourly pass places a reminder of its own at most an hour ahead with a key of thing, occurrence and window, and the job plans it again when it runs. So a moved date, a tick, a stop, a change of How many or a membership ended needs nothing cancelled, and no job payload holds a word.

**What can be reminded about, and to whom.** A thing's author alone, while an accepted member, about their own words: never a calendar's row, mail, a capture or an untrusted dump. A date another member set holds the author's reminders until the author edits the thing, so nobody can arrange for somebody else to be woken; a reminder of its own goes only between 7:00 and 22:00. The three sets the code decides on, what a pass may label, what a task may be for and which memories to date, are SQL functions in migration 0089 (`reminders_wanted`, `reminder_anchors`, `memories_to_date`), called by the daily run's booking, the worker's reads and the Core API's apply alike.

**A name reaches a lock screen, so it is never the model's words.** A note's or a memory's short name is accepted only as a run of the thing's own text, with no address and no four digits in a row; otherwise the copy uses the text's first words.

**Phones already installed** are sent nothing of their own: a token says at registration whether its build takes a reminder (`takes_reminders`), and `/api/me` keeps `todayLayoutV2` without the new Today section, adding `todayLayoutV3`, the precedent of 2026-10-07 one step on.

**The privacy page** said, before the first reminder, that offload works out what kind of date a thing is to remind about it, that reminders are among the notifications, that a notification may carry what offload remembers such as a birthday, and that somebody not on offload can have their birthday named in a reminder on another person's phone. Read and approved by the owner on 2026-10-10.

**Evals.** A new principle, `32-reminders.json`, 24 cases (the owner's examples, every kind and lead, the `none` cases, an anchored task, names as runs of the thing's own words), and four injection cases in `15-injected-text-is-data.json`: 24 of 24 and 4 of 4 on the first run, 2026-10-10, on Sonnet 5, about $0.05. There is no before: no prompt labelled anything until this.

**Design system impact:** see the design log's entry of the same day.

## .claude/skills/offload-design/decisions.md

### 2026-10-10 · Reminders: the Notifications section, the switch on every thing, Today's section, as built
**Surface:** iOS, Android, web

**Is:** the Notifications page gains a **Reminders** section between Dates and People, under the `bell` glyph: a switch, Reminders, with "Before the dates that need one, in those two messages or just before a set time."; then **How many** as three choice tiles, Low, Medium and High, each a small picture (a short line ending at a date mark, with one, two or three marks before it, drawn from tokens), with the caption under the row following the pick ("One for each date.", "Up to two for each date.", "Up to three for each date."). Off, the tiles and the caption fade as the hour rows already do and are not pressable. The morning and evening hours grey only when both switches that use them are off. Dates' caption becomes "What is due, in one message each morning and evening." The web's page summary adds "with reminders". The **Remind me** switch shows on the row edit sheet, the note editor and the memory editor for the author whenever offload will remind them; on the web it is the settings `Switch`, where the edit dialog had a checkbox. The date chip's bell follows `bellOn`: offload will remind, or somebody asked. **Today** gains a **Reminders** section under Your day with a count in its heading, as the other sections have: rows are the thread page's row; a task with no date of its own that belongs to a trip shows a plain chip "By 18 October" with the bell; notes and memories are a line of their name, a plain date chip, and their thread's pill, "Memories", or "Not in a thread". Customize Today lists it with its grip and switch. Activity's `reminder` row draws the `bell` glyph and opens its thread. Android gains a fifth channel, Reminders, named as its switch since the notification's title is a time.

**Where the code diverged, and why.** There is no mock for any of it, so every shape above is code-side. The tiles follow 031, which made every settings choice a tile; `SegmentedControl` stays for switching views. The Android tiles' selected ring uses `blue-600`, a ramp, as the existing tiles do: there is no Android accent alias. Phones and web differ in four places, logged rather than forced together: a note or memory line has a lead glyph on the web (`text-quote`, `cloudy`) and none on the phones; "and N more" sits inside the card on the web and under it on the phones; the note editor's switch is written when flipped on the web and folded into Save on the phones; the phones' Remind me row carries the bell glyph beside its label. The memory editor has no Undo, so neither does its switch. Toggling a note's switch alone toasts "Saved".

**Design system impact:** the readme's line that "a `bell` after the date means the digest will announce this row" (854) wants "offload will remind you about it, or somebody asked"; the line that the flag is the model's alone (862) and that Remind me shows only "while the row is a reminder" (918) want the author's switch on anything offload reminds about, in the row sheet, the note editor and the memory editor; the kits' "no per-item pushes" wants the reminder of its own before a clock time. The kits want: the Notifications page's Reminders section with the three count tiles and their picture; Today's Reminders section with the row, the "By" chip and the note and memory lines; Customize Today's fourth section; the web edit dialog's switch; the Activity `reminder` row; and a decision on the four phone and web differences above.
