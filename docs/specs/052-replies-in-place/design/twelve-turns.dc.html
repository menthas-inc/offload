Four directions

A. ObjectsThe reply is what was filed.

Each kind is drawn as itself: a to-do you can tick, an event as a date tile, a note in offload's voice, a memory, a new thread as its tile. Grouped under the thread, which is the one link out. Words only when there is something to tell.

For: native and actionable; AnswerResult extended to every turn, so most parts exist.

Against: a quiet filing now takes real height; a long dump is a long reply.

B. Sentenceoffload says what it did, and the nouns are live.

One sentence, written by code from the turn's record, never by the model. Each thing is a token you tap to open. Reads longer than three fall back to rows.

For: feels like a reply, lightest on screen, reads well aloud and in a notification.

Against: slower to scan; tokens are a new pattern; nothing tickable without opening.

C. In placeShow it where it landed.

A slice of the destination with the new part marked: the list with its neighbours, the week with the event in its slot, the Memories group, the thread grid. Edits show as movement.

For: teaches the filing; context answers "did it go to the right place" at a glance.

Against: heaviest to build, a mini renderer per layout; most pixels for a simple add.

D. ReceiptOne card per turn, one line per change.

A headline in offload's voice, then a ledger: glyph, what, where, and Undo on each line. Reads and sources go in the same card.

For: one shape for every turn; best for mixed dumps; Undo per change.

Against: uniform means a calendar never looks like a calendar; closest to today.

What every direction keeps

  • Words that name what was filed come from the record, not the model, as say and the answer prompt require now. The model still owns could_not_do, waiting and the answer to a question.
  • Silence is still allowed. A filing with nothing to tell shows its elements and no sentence padding.
  • Undo sits on the change, per 043, so a dump filed from the composer no longer needs the plate line.
  • A reply destroys nothing. Ticking stays the only write a row in a reply offers; no delete, archive or rename.
  • Moss for what offload did, blue for what needs you. A question back is the one blue thing in a reply.
  • No em dashes, no emoji, sentence case, and no noun for what was filed: counts stand alone.
